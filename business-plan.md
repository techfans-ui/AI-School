# Business Plan: VETAIML
### AI Training School for Military Veterans

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**VETAIML** is a workforce training organization that equips U.S. military veterans with practical artificial intelligence skills to transition into high-demand technology careers. We deliver accelerated, cohort-based AI training online and in-person, backed by VA education benefits and employer hiring partnerships.

- **Mission:** Bridge the gap between military service and the AI economy by giving veterans the skills, credentials, and connections to thrive in tech.
- **Target Market:** U.S. veterans and active-duty service members transitioning out of the military.
- **Revenue Model:** Tuition (VA/GI Bill funded), employer placement fees, corporate training contracts, and grant funding.
- **Launch Goal:** First cohort of 20 students within 6 months of VA approval.

---

## 2. Problem & Opportunity

- 200,000+ service members transition out of the military each year.
- AI/ML roles are the fastest-growing job category, with average salaries of $120,000–$180,000.
- Veterans have highly transferable skills (discipline, systems thinking, leadership) but lack AI-specific credentials.
- Existing coding bootcamps are not tailored to veteran experience, pace, or culture.
- VET TEC and GI Bill funding is underutilized in the AI training space.

---

## 3. Solution

VETAIML offers:
- **12-week intensive AI bootcamp** (full-time) and **24-week part-time track**
- Curriculum built around real-world AI tools used in defense, healthcare, logistics, and cybersecurity
- Veteran peer mentorship and military-style cohort culture
- Job placement support with committed hiring partners
- Pathways to Google, AWS, and Microsoft AI certifications

---

## 4. Target Market

**Primary:** Veterans transitioning from active duty (ages 22–40)
**Secondary:** Veterans already in the workforce seeking upskilling
**Tertiary:** Active-duty service members eligible for SkillBridge

**Market Size:**
- ~200,000 veterans transition annually
- ~2.5 million veterans are unemployed or underemployed
- VET TEC program approved ~15,000 enrollees per year (growing)

---

## 5. Business Model

| Revenue Stream | Details |
|---|---|
| **Tuition – GI Bill / VET TEC** | Primary revenue; VA pays tuition directly once approved |
| **Employer Placement Fees** | 10–15% of first-year salary from hiring partners |
| **Corporate AI Training** | B2B workshops for defense contractors and tech firms |
| **Grants & Donations** | Federal, foundation, and corporate grants (nonprofit track) |

**Pricing:**
- Full 12-week program: $12,000 (covered by VET TEC / Chapter 33)
- Part-time 24-week program: $8,000
- Individual courses: $500–$1,500

---

## 6. Legal Structure & VA Approval

**Recommended Structure:** Start as a for-profit LLC, with a parallel 501(c)(3) nonprofit arm for grant eligibility.

**VA Approval Steps:**
1. Register as a Licensing & Certification (L&C) program or proprietary school
2. Apply for State Approving Agency (SAA) approval in your state
3. Apply for VET TEC provider status through VA's vendor portal
4. Timeline: 6–12 months for full approval

## 7. Operations Plan

**Phase 1 – Months 1–6: Foundation**
- Incorporate LLC and begin nonprofit application
- Hire curriculum director and 1 lead instructor (veteran preferred)
- Develop and pilot curriculum with a free beta cohort of 10 students
- Apply for VA/VET TEC approval
- Build website and brand

**Phase 2 – Months 7–12: Launch**
- Launch first paid cohort (20 students) upon VA approval
- Establish 5+ employer hiring partners
- Begin generating placement fee revenue
- Apply for first round of federal and foundation grants

**Phase 3 – Year 2: Scale**
- Expand to 3 cohorts per year (60 students/year)
- Launch online-only track for national reach
- Add SkillBridge track for active-duty members
- Target $1M ARR
