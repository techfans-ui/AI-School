**Tone:** Cinematic, Military-Inspired, Motivational

## PRODUCTION NOTES
- **Music:** Cinematic/orchestral — start quiet, build through Scene 4, swell at Scene 7
- **Voiceover:** Male or female veteran voice — calm, authoritative, no hype
- **Color Grade:** Dark, desaturated military tones → shift to brighter, warmer tones in Scene 7+
- **Length:** Exactly 2:00 — can cut Scene 2 to 90-second version for social media
- **Caption Version:** All key stats should appear as on-screen text for silent autoplay
