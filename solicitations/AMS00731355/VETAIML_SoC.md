Executive Summary
VETAIML respectfully submits this Statement of Capabilities in response to NAVFAC Notice ID AMS00731355 (Notice of Intent to Award on a Sole Source Basis). We propose that opening competition for the delivery of an online, self‑study "AI in AEC" course for 450 students would be advantageous to the Government by increasing cost competitiveness, providing alternative delivery timelines, and offering additional student support and sustainment options.

Why Competition Is Advantageous
- Cost: We can provide competitive pricing, flexible licensing models (seat-based, subscription, or cohort), and volume discounts for 450 students.
- Schedule & Risk: We maintain ready-to-deploy LMS packaging and can onboard a cohort of 450 within 2–4 weeks, reducing schedule risk compared to a custom sole-source development.
- Capacity & Scalability: Our platform is architected to scale to large self‑study deployments with automated onboarding, reporting, and progress tracking.

Proposed High-Level Solution
- Deliverable: An online, self‑study "AI in AEC" course (asynchronous modules, assessments, downloadable materials) for 450 learners.
- LMS & Hosting: Hosted on a secure, scalable LMS with SSO, student progress tracking, and reporting for contracting officer access.
- Student Support: Email-based helpdesk, weekly office-hour drop-ins (optional), automated assessments and certificates.
- Timeline: Content delivery and LMS provisioning within 2–4 weeks of award; full access for 450 students within 30 days.

Capacity & Evidence
- Course development team with experience in AI and AEC domain content.
- Proven ability to run cohort and self‑study programs and to manage learner enrollment, support, and reporting at scale.
