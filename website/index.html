Funding Your Training

You've Earned This.
We'll Help You Use It.

Most veterans can attend VETAIML at zero out-of-pocket cost.

VET TEC Program

VA's dedicated tech training benefit covers tuition 100% and provides a housing stipend during training. We guide you through the application.

Learn about VET TEC →

Post-9/11 GI Bill (Ch. 33)

Covers tuition and fees up to the national maximum, plus a monthly housing allowance and books/supplies stipend.

Learn about GI Bill →

SkillBridge

Active-duty members within 180 days of separation can attend VETAIML through DoD SkillBridge — fully authorized.

Learn about SkillBridge →

Scholarships & Grants

Need-based and merit scholarships available for veterans not yet eligible for VA benefits. No one is turned away due to funding.

Meet the Founder

MS

Manse Soura

Founder & CEO — VETAIML LLC
U.S. Army Veteran AI/ML Engineer AWS Amazon Cloud Engineer Oracle SR Engineer

Manse Soura is a combat-decorated U.S. Army veteran and seasoned technology executive with a career spanning enterprise cloud infrastructure, site reliability engineering, and applied artificial intelligence. Following seven years of service — including a combat deployment — he transitioned into the technology sector, holding engineering roles at Oracle and Amazon before advancing to his current position as AI/ML Engineer at UMB Financial Corporation.

At UMB, Manse has architected and deployed four production-grade conversational AI systems and one autonomous AI agent, and is leading enterprise AI development on Amazon Bedrock, Amazon SageMaker, and Amazon Q Business. He holds a Master of Science in Big Data and Information Technology and a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics and Statistics.

Manse founded VETAIML to solve a problem he witnessed firsthand: veterans leaving service with exceptional discipline and systems-thinking skills, yet lacking access to the technical training needed to compete in the AI economy. VETAIML is his answer — a rigorous, military-structured program designed to close that gap, fully funded through GI Bill and VET TEC benefits.
