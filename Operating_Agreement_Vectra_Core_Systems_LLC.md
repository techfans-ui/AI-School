This Limited Liability Company Operating Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of the date of execution below, by the sole individual owner and Member listed herein.

ARTICLE I: IDENTITY & FORMATION
1.1 Formation. The Member has formed a limited liability company named Vectra Core Systems LLC (the "Company") by filing Articles of Organization with the Secretary of State in accordance with the laws of the State of formation.

1.2 Principal Office. The principal place of business and office of the Company shall be located at the address specified in the Company’s SAM.gov profile, or at such other place as the Member may determine.

1.3 Purpose. The purpose of the Company is to engage in any lawful act or activity for which a limited liability company may be organized under state law, including but not limited to, providing integrated technology, logistics systems, and federal contracting solutions.

ARTICLE II: VETERAN OWNERSHIP & CAPITAL
2.1 Sole Ownership. As of the effective date of this Agreement, [Insert Your Full Name] (the "Member") is a Service-Disabled Veteran and the sole equity owner of the Company, holding 100% of the membership and voting interests in the Company.

2.2 Capital Contributions. The Member has contributed initial capital to the Company as set forth in Exhibit A, attached hereto. The Member is not obligated to make any additional capital contributions, though they may choose to do so at their sole discretion.

ARTICLE III: ABSOLUTE AND UNCONDITIONAL MANAGEMENT
3.1 Management Structure. The Company shall be entirely Member-managed. The Member shall serve as the highest-ranking officer of the Company, holding the formal title of Managing Member and Chief Executive Officer (CEO).

3.2 Unconditional Control. In accordance with federal guidelines for Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Businesses (SDVOSBs):
- The Managing Member shall have absolute, full, and unconditional control over all day-to-day operations, corporate management decisions, financial choices, and strategic directions of the Company.
- The Managing Member possesses the exclusive, independent authority to execute contracts, sign federal proposals, bind the corporation, open and close banking accounts, hire or fire staff, and manage any lines of credit.
- No third party, non-veteran manager, consultant, employee, or outside entity shall possess any veto authority or voting power over the decisions of the Managing Member.

ARTICLE IV: FINANCIALS AND TAXATION
4.1 Allocations. All profits, losses, tax credits, and cash distributions of the Company shall be allocated 100% directly to the sole Member.

4.2 Taxation. For federal tax purposes, as a single-member LLC, the Company shall be treated as a disregarded entity unless the Member formally elects corporate taxation by filing IRS Form 8832.

ARTICLE V: INDEMNIFICATION & AMENDMENTS
5.1 Liability Limitation. The Member shall not be personally liable for any debts, obligations, or liabilities of the Company solely by reason of being a member or manager.

5.2 Amendments. This Agreement may be amended, modified, or replaced only by a written instrument explicitly executed and signed by the sole Member.
