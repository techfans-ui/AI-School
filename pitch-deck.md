## Slide 2 — The Problem

**200,000 veterans transition out of military service every year.**

- Most land in jobs that underutilize their skills
- Average veteran salary post-service is 30–40% below civilian peers
- The AI economy is creating 500,000+ new jobs annually — and veterans are missing out
- Existing bootcamps are not designed for veteran learning styles, schedules, or culture

> *"Veterans have world-class discipline, systems thinking, and leadership. They just lack one thing: the right technical credential."*

## Slide 4 — Our Solution

**VETAIML** is a 12-week, accelerated AI training program built specifically for veterans.

- ✅ Cohort-based, military-style structure
- ✅ Veteran-led instructors
- ✅ 100% GI Bill / VET TEC eligible
- ✅ 6 applied AI modules + 3 certification pathways
- ✅ Employer hiring partner network
- ✅ Job guarantee (or tuition refund)

## Slide 5 — How It Works

**The Student Journey (12 Weeks)**

```
Week 1–2    →   AI Foundations + Prompt Engineering
Week 3–4    →   Python for AI
Week 5–6    →   Data & AI Literacy
Week 7–8    →   Machine Learning (+ Interview Prep Begins)
Week 9–10   →   Generative AI & LLMs
Week 11–12  →   Real-World AI + Capstone + Employer Demo Day
              ↓
          Graduate → Certified → Hired
```

**Format:** Full-time (12 wk) or Part-time online (24 wk)
