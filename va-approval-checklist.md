# VA / VET TEC Approval Checklist
### Step-by-Step Guide to Becoming a VA-Approved AI Training Provider

---

## Overview

To accept GI Bill and VET TEC funding, your school must be approved by:
1. Your **State Approving Agency (SAA)** — required for all VA education programs
2. The **VA** directly — for VET TEC provider status

Timeline: **6–12 months** end-to-end. Start immediately.

---

## Phase 1 — Business Formation (Month 1)
*Must be complete before any approval applications*

- [ ] Register your LLC or corporation with your state Secretary of State
- [ ] Obtain a Federal EIN (Employer Identification Number) at irs.gov — free, instant
- [ ] Open a dedicated business bank account
- [ ] Obtain a DUNS number / register at SAM.gov (required for federal contracts and grants)
- [x] Obtain a DUNS number / register at SAM.gov (required for federal contracts and grants) — UEI: UHUMP38XMEU8 • CAGE: 219N8 (Active 2026-06-13)
- [ ] Secure a physical address (office, coworking space, or registered agent address)
- [ ] Create a professional website with program details, tuition costs, and school policies
- [ ] Draft your School Catalog (required by SAA) — includes:
  - [ ] Program descriptions and objectives
  - [ ] Tuition and fee schedule
  - [ ] Refund policy
  - [ ] Admissions requirements
  - [ ] Attendance and satisfactory academic progress policies
  - [ ] Grievance/complaint procedures
  - [ ] Faculty qualifications

## Phase 2 — State Approving Agency (SAA) Approval (Months 2–5)
*Required before VA can approve you. Each state has its own SAA.*

### Find Your SAA
- Directory: https://nasaa-vetseducation.com/contacts/
- Contact them early — they are helpful and will guide you

### Required Documents (varies by state — confirm with your SAA)
- [ ] Completed SAA application form
- [ ] School Catalog (see Phase 1)
- [ ] Proof of business registration
- [ ] Surety bond (amount varies by state, typically $10,000–$50,000)
- [ ] Financial statements or proof of financial stability
- [ ] Facility inspection (if in-person program)
- [ ] Instructor qualifications and resumes
- [ ] Sample curriculum and course syllabi
- [ ] Student enrollment agreement template
- [ ] Accreditation status (or exemption documentation)
- [ ] Application fee (varies by state)

### Key SAA Notes
- For **online-only programs**: You may need SAA approval in every state where students reside (SARA membership simplifies this — see below)
- **SARA (State Authorization Reciprocity Agreement)**: Join NC-SARA to operate in all member states with one approval. Apply at nc-sara.org.
- [ ] Apply for NC-SARA membership (if offering online programs nationally)

## Phase 3 — VET TEC Provider Application (Months 4–8)
*Submit after or alongside SAA approval*

### What is VET TEC?
VA's Veteran Employment Through Technology Education Courses program pays 100% of tuition for approved high-tech training programs including AI, ML, data science, and software development.

### Eligibility Requirements
- Must have at least one active VET TEC-eligible program (AI/ML qualifies)
- Programs must lead to employment in a high-technology industry
- Must have SAA approval (or be in process)
- Must demonstrate job placement capabilities

### Application Steps
1. [ ] Register at VA's vendor portal: https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/how-to-use-benefits/vettec-high-tech-program/
2. [ ] Complete VA Form 22-10280 (VET TEC Provider Agreement)
3. [ ] Submit program details:
   - [ ] Program name, description, and learning objectives
   - [ ] Duration and format (in-person, online, hybrid)
   - [ ] Tuition and fee schedule
   - [ ] Admissions requirements
   - [ ] Completion and certification outcomes
4. [ ] Provide evidence of employer partnerships / hiring outcomes
5. [ ] Submit instructor credentials
6. [ ] Sign VA Provider Agreement
7. [ ] Await VA review and site visit (if applicable)

### VA Contact
- VET TEC Program Office: 1-888-442-4551
- Email: VETTEC.VBABUF@va.gov

## Phase 4 — GI Bill Chapter 33 Approval (concurrent with Phase 3)

- [ ] Once SAA approved, VA automatically reviews for Chapter 33 (Post-9/11 GI Bill)
- [ ] Ensure your enrollment certifying official (SCO) is designated and trained
  - [ ] Complete VA SCO training at https://www.benefits.va.gov/gibill/resources/education_resources/school_certifying_officials/
  - [ ] Register your SCO in VA's WEAMS system
- [ ] Set up VA enrollment certification process for students

---

## Phase 5 — SkillBridge Partnership (Month 6+)

SkillBridge allows active-duty members within 180 days of separation to train with you for free (DoD pays their salary).

- [ ] Register as a SkillBridge provider at https://skillbridge.osd.mil/
- [ ] Complete SkillBridge Partnership Agreement with DoD
- [ ] Coordinate with local installation Transition Assistance Program (TAP) offices
- [ ] Create a SkillBridge-specific program description and schedule

---

## Phase 6 — Ongoing Compliance

- [ ] File annual enrollment reports with SAA
- [ ] Maintain student outcome data (completion rate, job placement rate, salary data)
- [ ] Update school catalog annually
- [ ] Notify SAA of any program changes (tuition, curriculum, format)
- [ ] Renew surety bond annually
- [ ] Stay current on VA regulatory changes (subscribe to VA education updates)

## Pro Tips

1. **Call your SAA first.** They are your best resource and will tell you exactly what they need. Be transparent that you are a new school.
2. **Get a veteran education attorney** for initial review of your school catalog and enrollment agreement — $500–$1,500 well spent.
3. **Start with a self-pay pilot cohort** while waiting for approval. Use outcomes data (completion rates, job placements) to strengthen your VA application.
4. **Document everything.** VA reviewers want evidence of outcomes. Track every student from day one.
5. **Join CECU or APTD** — trade associations for proprietary schools that provide regulatory guidance and advocacy.
